# About me

I am a machine learning platform engineer who enjoys building, repairing, and understanding systems. I currently work at NVIDIA. Previously, I worked at Salesforce as a Member of Technical Staff, first with the Industries Surveys team and later as an ML engineer with the Detection and Response team.

I completed my M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay in 2019 and my bachelor's degree in Engineering at Jawaharlal Nehru Engineering College, Aurangabad. At IIT Bombay I worked as a Research Assistant with the CSE system administrators. My thesis, “Fake News Detection: Fact Checking and Bias Detection,” was advised by [Prof. S. Sudarshan](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~sudarsha) and co-advised by [Prof. Soumen Chakrabarti](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~soumen). [Read the final report]({{ '/pdfs/mtp_report.pdf' | relative_url }}).

My main technical interests are NLP, machine learning, system administration, electronics, 3D printing, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi projects. Outside technology, I enjoy trekking, cycling, books, films, and travel. I have also written film reviews for the IIT Bombay elective “Perspectives on World Cinema”; [the collected reviews are available as a PDF]({{ '/pdfs/DE_410.pdf' | relative_url }}).
